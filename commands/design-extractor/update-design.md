# Update Design System

Guide the user through updating design.md with a fresh extraction.

## Process

1. Tell the user: "To update design.md, re-run the Design.md Extractor Chrome extension on your site"
2. Provide steps:
   - Open the site in Chrome
   - Click the Design.md Extractor icon
   - Click "Extract Design System"
   - Click "Download" to get a new ZIP
   - Run `bash install.sh` from your project directory to replace design.md
3. After they confirm the update, read the new @design.md and summarize what changed
